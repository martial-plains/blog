I'm Allister. I learnt Swedish first and then turned to Icelandic, because I
wanted to know where the language I already spoke had come from.

I write about Icelandic, Emacs and Org mode, and about the odd projects that
grow out of them. I'm the author of /Icelandic Vocabulary: A Learner's Companion
to the Words, Grammar and Culture of Iceland/.
